Why these moments, not more in-funnel: today’s factor_plus_in_funnel pulse (14 Oct) shows adoption +22.4% but gross activations −5.05% (seq-significant), about 1.9 lost activations per extra adopter. All 5 moments above sit after activation (account, tracking, plan settings, cancel, hub), so they can’t repeat that trade. Keep gross activations as a hard guardrail on anything that touches new-customer checkout (E7).
Considered, not prototyped: household / family sharing (Instacart, talabat). It adds cost and fraud risk until the package is cheaper (SCAMPER C3). Student / SNAP tiers (Shipt, DashPass) need eligibility tooling. Partner perks and sponsored offers (Uber Eats × Hulu, Deliveroo × Amazon Music, Skip × Apple Arcade): category evidence says perk catalogues get cut, and sponsored offers would compete with the Factor Plus card for the same space. Gift a membership: low volume.
