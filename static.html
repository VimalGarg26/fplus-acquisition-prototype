A · Join from the cart without leaving it

Today the cart checkbox doesn’t add anything. It replaces “All done!” with a link out of the cart, then leads through a join page and a modal with annual $99 preselected. The proposal: one sheet opens over the cart, with the monthly plan preselected, the saved PayPal and one button.
Hypothesis: cart entry→member ≥ +30% relative · kill if <10% after 2 wks · refund-48h ≤ +0.5pp
Today
5 taps + 1 page changetick → See benefits → Join Annual → tick consent → Join
  1. 1Ticking the checkbox navigates away instead of adding Factor Plus
  2. 2“All done!” disappears, so the user can’t finish the order
  3. 3Annual $99 is the default. “Save 67%” here conflicts with “70%” in the modal
  4. 4“$80 a month” is a fourth savings number, with no basis given
  5. 5“Risk-free” and “nonrefundable” appear in the same modal; the refund window is 48h and only if benefits are unused
  6. 6A required consent checkbox blocks the button

B · Post-order interstitial: keep what works, fix the leaks

The Q3 interstitial is Factor’s best surface: it shows up after the order is saved, itemises the savings and leads with the per-week price. But it sells monthly, then hands off to a “next step” where annual is preselected, and it shares the modal with a Meal Shake offer.
Hypothesis: interstitial→member ≥ +15% relative vs Q3 design · guardrail: accidental sign-up ≤1% of adopters
Q3 design
~3–4 tapsAdd → next step → consent → Join
  1. 1A second offer (Meal Shake) competes in the same modal
  2. 2“+$12.99” and “+$20.00” have plus signs, so they read as charges
  3. 3“New box total” doesn’t show the old total
  4. 4“No thanks, keep paying for shipping” is mild confirmshaming
  5. 5“Review your plan on the next step” leads to a different offer (annual preselected)
  6. 6Naming drift: “Factor plus” in lower case, “premium upgrades / wk”

Hypothesis: zero-Premium-redemption 44% → ≤30% within 4 boxes · 8-wk AOR ≥ +1pp · kill if >40% (rename the benefit)

D · Entry points + one savings claim

There are 7 entry points with 7 different lines. The home bar sits below supplements, and the Benefits page frames Factor Plus as LOCKED. The proposal: use one claim everywhere, show the maths, and move the pitch above the fold only for prospects whose orders show they’d benefit. Do this after A, so the extra traffic doesn’t land in today’s 5-tap flow.
Hypothesis: targeted home entry CTR ≥2× · add-on attach ≥ −3% relative · one-claim join conversion ≥ +10%
Today
4 savings numbers$12.99 · up to $32 · $80/mo · 67% vs 70%
  1. 1The card is framed as LOCKED, with lock icons: a benefit withheld rather than gained
  2. 2“Free standard shipping” is a fourth name for the shipping benefit
  3. 3No number and no reason to act now
  4. 4A second Factor Plus card is repeated below

(6) Brand check on the F+ badge colour (black today; green in the mockup to mark membership states).
